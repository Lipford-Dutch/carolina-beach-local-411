# Carolina Beach Local Vendors and Beach Vibes 411 Charter

## Purpose

Carolina Beach Local Vendors and Beach Vibes 411 is a fast, mobile-first local
guide for visitors and locals who want a practical beach-day plan without
opening ten tabs.

## Product Principles

- Keep the first screen action-oriented: search, filter, and build a route.
- Prefer source-backed local details over generic travel copy.
- Make vendor stubs honest when the public record is incomplete.
- Store saved routes and swag concepts only on the visitor's device.
- Keep the site accessible, responsive, and readable in short sessions.

## Current Scope

- Local vendor and service directory.
- Three-stop beach-day route builder.
- Beach 411 essentials for parking, access, safety, marina, and ramps.
- Local intel links for visitor information, harbor details, and news context.
- Early swag concept saves for future merchandising work.

## Out Of Scope

- Paid checkout, live inventory, booking, or account creation.
- Scraping private social-media content into the site.
- Publishing unverified operating hours as guaranteed.
- Reusing unrelated PDFs, memories, or prior project content.

## Content Governance

All business, municipal, and visitor guidance should link to a source page.
Where the user provides local knowledge that cannot be independently verified,
label the entry as pending verification instead of presenting it as official.
