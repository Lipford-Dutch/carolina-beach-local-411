# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must keep collaboration respectful, factual, and focused on the
project. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, and
deliberate disruption are not accepted.

## Project-specific expectations

- Protect private contact, customer, and account information.
- Do not publish unsupported claims about local businesses or public agencies.
- Correct inaccurate civic, safety, access, or vendor information promptly.
- Keep review feedback about the work, not the person.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the
Lipford-Dutch organisation. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
temporarily limit participation, or permanently remove participants when
necessary to protect the project and its community.
